The overwhelming response at Himos demonstrates the critical need for AI-assisted communication solutions among professional service companies. Operations leaders, communications managers, and PMO heads from across Finland discovered how Lyyli transforms internal coordination, eliminates communication friction, and ensures critical messages never go undelivered.

"We knew professional service organizations were struggling with communication overload," said Mikko Oksanen, CEO and Co-founder of Lyyli.ai. "But the response at Himos exceeded all our expectations. One thousand companies in one weekend isn't just a number – it's validation that we're solving a real, urgent problem."

## What Drove the Breakthrough

The rapid adoption was driven by three key factors:

**1. Live Demonstrations That Resonated**

Attendees saw firsthand how Lyyli integrates seamlessly with Slack, Microsoft Teams, and email to ensure important communications are never buried in channel overload. Professional service organizations could instantly calculate their potential savings. With an average team of 50 people saving 2 hours per week on communication tasks at 60€/hour, the ROI was undeniable: 2,400€ monthly savings with a 340% annual return on investment.

In an industry where trust is everything, Lyyli's AES-256 encryption, EU data centers, and full GDPR compliance addressed the critical security concerns that professional service organizations face. No compromises on data protection or compliance.
