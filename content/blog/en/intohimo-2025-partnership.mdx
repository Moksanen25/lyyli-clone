# Lyyli.ai Partners with Finnish Junior Chambers – Join Us at Intohimo 2025 Seminar to Make Your Problems Bigger

We're excited to announce our partnership with the **[Finnish Junior Chambers (Suomen Nuorkauppakamarit)](https://nuorkauppakamarit.fi)**. Lyyli.ai will be present at the **[Intohimo 2025](https://www.intohimo2025.fi)** autumn seminar, where our founders **Mikko Oksanen** and **Veikko Laitinen** will host a workshop on AI and the transformation of business communication.


## Come Make Your Problems Bigger!

### AI (Champagne) Workshop – When Your Business Needs a New Spark

**When?** Saturday 11:30–13:00 (arrive whenever suits you best)  
**Where?** Koulu, Kuvaamataito


## What happens?

### 🥂 Champagne and spontaneous mingling
Welcome warmly, glass in hand and thoughts ready to share!

### 🤖 "AI's Useless Use Cases" quick look
Mikko and Veikko present case examples that will help you see opportunities in your own business. We'll also share mistakes and cases where AI has done more harm than good.

### 🏥 Problem clinic
Bring your problem to the table, leave with guaranteed bigger problems! (But hopefully solutions too.)

### 💬 The communication revolution
How is AI changing the way we communicate?

### 🎯 "Lyyli vs. GPT-4" – live demo
See with your own eyes how Lyyli.ai captures your company's voice.

## Partnership Continues Beyond the Seminar

In addition to the Intohimo 2025 seminar, the **Finnish Junior Chambers will use the Lyyli.ai service free of charge**. This partnership provides us with valuable insights for service development – through user experiences and feedback, we can make Lyyli.ai an even better tool for Finnish business communication.

"The Junior Chamber network represents exactly the target audience Lyyli.ai is designed for – active, future-oriented leaders and entrepreneurs who understand the potential of technology in their business," states **Mikko Oksanen**.


## Join Us at Intohimo!

The Finnish Junior Chambers' national autumn conference **Intohimo 2025** will be held **October 10-12, 2025** in Himos, Jämsä. The event is a unique opportunity to delve into emotional intelligence in the workplace, the significance of diversity, and the power of passion.
