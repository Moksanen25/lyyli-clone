# Lyyli.ai's updates and bug fixes: New language models, Teams integration, and Nano Banana Pro

Last week has brought significant improvements to Lyyli. Our CTO Veikko Laitinen has delivered a set of updates that make communication even smoother and more efficient for our customers.

## New Language Models: GPT-5.1 and Gemini 3

Two of the latest language models are now available at the core of content production: **GPT-5.1** and **Gemini 3**. Both offer even more accurate and natural text that genuinely sounds like you.

## Nano Banana Pro: Brand Visuals in Image Generation

The newest version of Nano Banana, **Nano Banana Pro**, brings a feature our customers have been asking for: **brand colors and fonts directly in image prompts**.

You can now input your company's brand colors and fonts in settings and reference them directly in prompts. This significantly speeds up and streamlines image creation. The previous version couldn't follow instructions as literally, but the Pro version delivers exactly what you ask for.

![Lyyli.ai visual identity settings – screenshot of the new menu](/images/blog/Lyyli_UI_visual_settings.webp)

Additionally, Nano Banana Pro includes fixes for minor bugs and an improved user experience.

## User Experience Improvements

Small details that make daily work easier:

- **Enter command sends the message in chat** – line breaks now happen with **Shift+Enter**

- **Em dash can be turned off** directly in settings (no more em dash haunting!)

- **Adding stock images as reference images** now works with **Drag&Drop**

## Teams Integration Now Available

Especially larger organizations have requested **Microsoft Teams integration** – and now it's here.

Teams integration works **bidirectionally** just like Slack:

- You can set Lyyli to monitor only specific channels

- Choose separately which channels allow publishing

- Lyyli works as your team's communication assistant where the work already happens
