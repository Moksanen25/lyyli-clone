# ANNEX 1 – Service Description (SaaS)

**Service:** Lyyli.ai – AI-assisted communication assistant for internal and external communications.

## Key features

- Multichannel content drafting (Teams, Slack, email, social)
- Scheduling & distribution with Customer authorisations
- Tools for ideation, summaries and highlights
- Multilingual support and brand/tone controls
- User & role management (Admin, Editor, Viewer)
- Integrations: Microsoft 365/Teams, Slack, email, HubSpot (as applicable)

## Integrations and Third Parties

- The Service may integrate with third-party platforms (such as LinkedIn, Microsoft Teams, Slack).
- Third-party services are not under Lyyli.ai's control. Lyyli.ai is not responsible for the availability or changes of these services (e.g. API changes).
- When a user connects an integration, Lyyli.ai processes data only with user authorization and for the purpose of providing the Service.

## Limitations & Acceptable Use

- **Content Responsibility:** Customer is responsible for content accuracy and final approvals. Customer is also responsible for ensuring that content published to third-party platforms via Lyyli.ai complies with the rules and terms of those platforms.
- **Authorizations:** Automations and integrations operate only under Customer-granted permissions.
- **Suspension of Integration:** Lyyli.ai reserves the right to block or suspend the use of an integration if its use compromises security, violates third-party terms, or causes harm to the Service.
- **Fair Use:** Fair-use limits defined per account/plan. The Service may not be used for automated mass messaging (spam) or other activities violating platform terms.
