# ANNEX 6 – Data Retention & Deletion

**Retention periods (unless otherwise required by law):**
- User account data: term + 30 days  
- Content & message drafts: default **12 months** (Customer may define otherwise)  
- Logs: **180 days**  
- Backups: **30-day** rolling retention

**Deletion process:** primary data deleted promptly upon request; backups overwritten after the retention period. A deletion certificate is provided upon request.
